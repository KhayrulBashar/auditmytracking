About AuditMyTracking

Accurate tracking is the foundation of every profitable marketing decision. We built AuditMyTracking to make professional-grade tracking audits accessible to everyone, in seconds.

Who is behind this tool

AuditMyTracking is built and maintained by Web Analytics Solution (WAS), a specialist web analytics agency. Our team brings over four years of hands-on experience and more than 100 completed tracking projects for businesses around the world.

We work every day with Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4, Google Ads conversion tracking, Meta Conversions API, Consent Mode v2, and server-side tracking with Stape. That real-world expertise is built directly into the checks this tool runs.

What the tool does

AuditMyTracking scans any public website and reports what tracking is installed and what may be missing or misconfigured across GA4, Google Ads, Meta Pixel and CAPI, GTM, Consent Mode v2, and server-side tagging. It is designed to give you a clear, technical starting point in seconds, at no cost.

Why it matters

Broken or missing tracking quietly wastes ad budget and hides the true performance of your campaigns. A single missed conversion tag can distort every optimization decision that follows. Our goal is to help you find those gaps early and fix them with confidence.
